1. What UI Recorder does

UI Recorder has two capture modes. Pick the one that fits the job:

Start Recording

Records a video of a browser tab, an application window, or your whole screen.

Output: a .webm video file.

Good for: demos, walkthroughs, meetings.

Start Step-by-Step Capture

Takes a screenshot at every click, highlights what you clicked, and writes a caption such as “Click Save”.

Output: a numbered guide (index.html plus one PNG per step).

Good for: how-to guides, procedures (SOPs), bug reports.

Both modes are started from the toolbar icon. Nothing is recorded until you press a start button.

2. Install

  1. Open the Chrome Web Store and search for UI Recorder.
  2. Click Add to Chrome, then Add extension.
  3. Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar and pin UI Recorder so its icon stays visible.

No account or sign-up is needed.

3. Open the menu

Click the UI Recorder icon in the toolbar.

  • Start Recording — video of a tab, window or screen.
  • Start Step-by-Step Capture — a screenshot for every click.
  • Review captured steps (N) — appears only when a step capture is waiting for you to review it.

4. Record a video

  1. Click Start Recording.
  2. Chrome asks what to share. Choose Chrome Tab, Window or Entire Screen, select the one you want, and click Share.
    • To include sound, tick Also share tab audio (for a tab) or Also share system audio (for a screen).
  3. Work as normal. A small floating control appears in the top-right corner of the page you started from:
    • Pause / Resume — hold the recording while you set something up, then continue. Paused time is not included in the video.
    • Stop — finish the recording. You can also press Esc.
  4. When you stop, the video is saved automatically. See Find and share your files.
Note The floating controls are semi-transparent so they stay out of the way. They become fully visible when you hover over them. When you record a window or the whole screen, the controls stay on the page you started from. If you move to another application, press Esc or use Stop in the toolbar menu.

5. Capture a step-by-step guide

  1. Open the page where your process begins, and log in before you start.
  2. Click the UI Recorder icon, then Start Step-by-Step Capture.
  3. A small Pause and Stop control appears on the page. Now do the task: click through it the way you normally would.
  4. UI Recorder takes a screenshot at every click, draws a highlight box around the thing you clicked, and writes a caption such as “Click Save”.

What the capture handles for you

  • Pages that are still loading. If a click opens a new page, the screenshot for that step is taken once the page has finished loading, not while it is blank.
  • Pop-ups and dialogs. When a click opens a dialog, a drawer or a cookie banner, the step shows it open.
  • Chat widgets and embedded content inside iframes are recorded too.
  • Single-page apps and multi-page sites. The capture keeps going as you move between pages in the same tab.

Pause and resume

Click the Pause button on the page when you need to do something you do not want in the guide, for example signing in with personal details. Click Resume to continue. Clicks made while paused are not captured.

Finish

Click Stop on the page, press Esc, or use Stop in the toolbar menu. Your steps are not exported yet. The review page opens next.

Privacy Only clicks are recorded. What you type is never stored, and password fields are never read. If a screenshot would show something sensitive, delete that step on the review page.

6. Review your steps

After you stop, the review page opens with every screenshot as a card.

To…Do this
ReorderDrag a card to a new position (a blue bar shows where it will land), or use the ◀ ▶ buttons on the card. Step numbers update as you move.
DeleteClick ✕ on the card. An Undo link stays for 8 seconds.
Zoom inClick a screenshot to see it at full size. Press Esc or click again to close.
GenerateClick Generate guide. The guide is written in the order shown, numbered 1, 2, 3 …
Start overClick Discard all. The captured steps are deleted.
Tip Your edits are saved as you make them. If you close the review tab by accident, open the toolbar menu and click Review captured steps (N) to return to it. You cannot start a new step capture until the pending one is generated or discarded.

7. Find and share your files

Everything is saved in your browser’s Downloads folder, inside a folder called recordings:

Downloads/
└── recordings/
    ├── recording-2026-10-19T07-35-27.webm     ← video recordings
    └── steps-2026-10-19T07-40-02/             ← one folder per guide
        ├── index.html                          ← open this to read the guide
        ├── step-01.png
        ├── step-02.png
        └── …

The generated guide is an ordinary web page. Each step shows its number, caption and screenshot:

Sharing a guide

  • Print or save as PDF — open index.html, press Ctrl+P (Cmd+P on a Mac), then choose Save as PDF.
  • Send it — zip the whole steps-… folder and email or upload it. Keep index.html and the PNG files together, or the pictures will not show.
  • Edit the wording — open index.html in any text editor and change the captions.

Playing a video

Videos are saved in WebM format, an open video format. Drag the file into a browser tab to play it. If another player does not open it, convert the file to MP4 with a free converter.

8. Tips for great guides

  • Click deliberately and pause briefly after each click, so the page can settle. One clean click per action works best.
  • Start on the right page. Open it and log in before you click Start.
  • Use Pause for sign-ins, personal data, or anything you do not want in the guide.
  • Close pop-ups with their own close button, not Esc. Pressing Esc stops the capture.
  • Use a normal-sized window. Screenshots show what the tab displays, so a normal window gives readable pictures.
  • Review everything. Delete accidental clicks and reorder steps before you generate the guide.
  • Hide sensitive tabs and bookmarks before recording a window or the whole screen. A video shows exactly what is on screen.

9. Things to know

  • Step capture records one tab, the one you started it in. If a click opens a new tab or window, that new tab is not followed. Go back to the original tab, or start a new capture there.
  • Some pages cannot be recorded. Chrome does not let extensions run on chrome:// pages, on other extensions’ pages, or on the Chrome Web Store, so step capture cannot run there. Video recording of a whole screen or window still works.
  • Clicks only. Typing, scrolling and hovering are not recorded as steps.
  • Esc stops the capture. It is a quick way out, so it also triggers when you are trying to close a pop-up.
  • Screenshot speed. Chrome limits how many screenshots an extension can take per second. If you click very quickly, screenshots may be slightly delayed, but every click is still kept.
  • Long recordings create large files.

10. Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to try
Nothing happens when I click StartMake sure you are on a normal web page, not a chrome:// page or the Web Store. Refresh the page and try again.
The sharing dialog does not appearThe dialog appears only for Start Recording. Step capture does not need it.
A step screenshot shows the page before it loadedWait for the page to settle after a click before clicking again. If a site keeps loading forever (for example live chat or analytics), the screenshot is taken after a short wait.
A pop-up is missing from the guideMake sure the pop-up opened because of your click. Close it with its own button, not Esc.
I closed the review page by accidentClick the toolbar icon, then Review captured steps.
I cannot start a new step captureA previous capture is still waiting. Open it from the menu and click Generate guide or Discard all.
I cannot find my filesOpen the downloads list (Ctrl+J) or your Downloads folder, then open the recordings folder.
The guide’s images are missingKeep index.html in the same folder as the step-NN.png files. Do not move them separately.

11. Privacy

  • Everything stays on your computer. UI Recorder has no servers, no accounts and no analytics.
  • What is captured: video and audio of the tab, window or screen you choose; screenshots and short click captions while a step capture runs; and the click position used to draw the highlight.
  • What is not captured: keystrokes, text you type, password fields, the addresses of the pages you visit, or your browsing history.
  • Where it goes: only to the Downloads folder you can see. Steps are held temporarily in the extension’s local storage until you generate or discard them.

The full privacy policy is provided with the extension listing.
