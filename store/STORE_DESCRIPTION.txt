UI Recorder: record your screen, or turn any click-through into a step-by-step guide.

Create how-to guides, tutorials, procedures and demo videos in minutes. UI Recorder is a lightweight Chrome extension with two ways to capture your work:

• Record a video of a browser tab, an application window or your whole screen.
• Click through a process and get a numbered guide with a screenshot for every step. Each click is highlighted and described for you.

There is nothing to set up. No account, no sign-up, and nothing is uploaded anywhere. Your recordings and guides are saved on your own computer.


WHY INSTALL UI RECORDER

Writing a how-to guide by hand means taking screenshots, cropping them, numbering them and describing every step again. UI Recorder does that while you work: do the task once, stop, review the steps, and share the result.

It handles the parts of the web that trip up simpler tools:

• It waits for the page to finish loading. A click that opens a new page is captured once the page is ready, not as a blank white screen.
• It captures pop-ups and dialogs. Menus, drawers, cookie banners and modal windows that open after a click appear in your guide.
• It captures chat widgets and embedded content. Clicks inside iframes, including support chat windows, are recorded.
• It follows you across pages. If a click loads a new page in the same tab, the capture keeps going.


FEATURES

1. Video recording
• Record a browser tab, an application window or the entire screen.
• Pause and resume during a recording, so you keep only the parts you want.
• Stop with the on-page Stop button or the Esc key.
• Include tab or system audio when Chrome asks what to share.
• The result is a .webm video saved to your Downloads folder.

2. Step-by-step capture
• Start the capture and work through the process as you normally would.
• A screenshot is taken at every click, with the clicked element outlined in red.
• Each step gets a plain-English caption, such as "Click Save" or "Click Email", so you don't have to write them.
• Steps stay in the order you clicked them, even when a page takes a moment to load.
• Pause at any time to leave out sensitive or unwanted steps.

3. Review before you publish
When you stop a step capture, a review page opens with every step as a card:
• Drag steps into a new order, or use the arrow buttons on each card.
• Delete any step you don't need. An Undo button stays available for a few seconds.
• Click any screenshot to see it at full size.
• Nothing is written to your computer until you press Generate guide.
• Your edits are saved as you go. If you close the review tab, reopen it from the toolbar menu.

4. A guide you can share anywhere
A generated guide is a simple web page (index.html) with a numbered screenshot for each step. Open it in any browser, print it to PDF, or zip the folder and send it to a colleague. You can edit the captions in any text editor.

PRIVACY

Your data stays with you.

• Recordings, screenshots and guides are saved only on your computer.
• UI Recorder does not send anything to its developer or to any third party.
• It does not need an account, and it contains no analytics or advertising.
• Typed text is never recorded, and password fields are never read. Only your clicks are captured.
• Your browsing history, cookies and the addresses of the pages you visit are not stored.

HOW TO USE IT

1. Open the page where your process starts, and sign in first if needed.
2. Click the UI Recorder icon, then choose Start Recording or Start Step-by-Step Capture.
3. Do the task. Use the floating Pause and Stop controls on the page when you need them.
4. Stop the capture. For a step guide, review the steps, then click Generate guide.
5. Find your files in Downloads, in the recordings folder.


LIMITATIONS

These limits help you decide whether UI Recorder fits your task:

• Step capture follows the browser tab where it started. If a click opens a new tab or window, that tab is not included.
• Chrome does not allow extensions to run on chrome:// pages, on other extensions' pages or on the Chrome Web Store, so those pages cannot be captured.
• Step capture records clicks. It does not record typing or scrolling.
• Pressing Esc ends a capture, so close pop-ups with their own close button while you are recording.
